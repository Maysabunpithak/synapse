# Leftward sunglasses gesture

`synapse-house-swagger-v1.png` was created with built-in Imagegen using the
existing house activity sheet as identity reference. HouseSwagger aligns its
four poses and mirrors the right-facing generated art to face left.
During each leftward stroll the house stops for 2.8 seconds: touch glasses,
lower them, remove and wink, put them on again, then continue walking.
The pose layers crossfade over 160 ms. Voice and touch take priority; reduced
motion does not enter this idle gesture. No sparkles were added.

## Final prompt

Use case: identity-preserve. Create a transparent animation sprite sheet EXACTLY 2 columns by 2 rows, four equal square cells. Reference is character identity: cream living house, orange pitched roof and cream chimney, brown sunglasses, cream thin arms with mitten hands, thin sandy legs with brown shoes. NOT a robot. Render exactly same cute soft 3D character, same size, lighting, architecture and proportions. All four full-body poses face three-quarter LEFT (opposite direction to reference). Roof top at 40px and feet baseline at 465px within each 512px cell, centered x256, safe margin, consistent body anchor. Frame 0 top-left: standing stopped mid-stroll, one hand gently grasps the temple of the still-worn sunglasses. Frame 1 top-right: hand lowers glasses just below eyes while giving a cool confident sideways look. Frame 2 bottom-left: glasses COMPLETELY off face held in one hand next to chest, eyes clearly uncovered, playful wink and small smug smile, other hand on hip, slight confident lean. Frame 3 bottom-right: glasses back on face, hand released down, relaxed cool standing pose ready to continue walking. The SAME sunglasses one pair only. No extra arms. Transparent alpha background, no stars, NO sparkles, no text, no grid, no shadows outside character. Exactly four aligned frames.
