# Synapse interior photography

`interior-living.jpg` and `interior-bedroom.jpg` are original AI-generated
decorative photographs created for the Synapse mobile redesign on 2026-09-30.
They are not camera feeds or photographs of a user's actual home.

The composition uses cream linen, walnut furniture, sand plaster, paper pendant
lights and warm natural light to complement the existing Synapse palette.
Each image is bundled locally at 1200 × 800, JPEG quality 84 (about 126 KB and
146 KB), so room cards do not depend on a third-party image host.

UI layout inspiration:
[Elegant Smart Home App by Digcy](https://dribbble.com/shots/27291362-App-Store-Screenshots-Elegant-Smart-Home-App).
The reference artwork is not shipped in the app.
